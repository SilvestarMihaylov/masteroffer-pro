# Privacy Policy

**Master Offer Pro**
**Last updated: 20 August 2026**

## 1. Who we are

Master Offer Pro (“we”, “the App”) helps professionals create price quotations, PDF documents and, where available on the applicable plan, invoices.

The App may also provide AI-assisted features for generating and organising offer content, as well as optional notifications and reminders related to subscriptions, invoices and other information entered or created by the user.

This Policy applies to the Master Offer Pro mobile app available through Google Play and the App Store.

The controller of personal data is the operator of Master Offer Pro. For contact, use the developer email published on the Google Play listing for the App.

* email address;
* display name;
* authentication identifiers, including Google Sign-In where used;
* unique user ID (UID);
* registration date;
* last sign-in information.

### Business profile

Depending on the information you choose to provide:

* notes;
* services;

Master Offer Pro may provide AI-assisted features that allow you to generate or structure offer content based on text or images provided by you.

Depending on the feature, the information processed may include:

* images uploaded for AI processing;

Master Offer Pro may provide optional notifications and reminders related to your account and content created in the App.

Depending on the features you use, reminders may relate to:

* partially paid invoices;

We may process information relating to your subscription and entitlements through Google Play Billing, including:

* subscription product;
* subscription term;

### Settings

### Technical information

We may process basic technical information necessary to operate, secure and improve the service, including:
