**Privacy Policy**
**Master Offer Pro** · Last updated: 13 August 2026

**1. Who we are**

Master Offer Pro (“we”, “the App”) helps professionals create price quotations, PDF documents and (where available on the applicable plan) invoices. This Policy applies to the Master Offer Pro mobile app on Google Play and the App Store.

The controller of personal data is the operator of Master Offer Pro. For contact, use the developer email published on the Google Play listing for the App.

**2. Data we collect**

Depending on how you use the service, we may process:

Account: email, display name, authentication identifiers (including Google Sign-In where used), unique user ID (UID), registration date and last sign-in.
Business profile: company or individual details, name, address, phone, email, logo or photo, entity type, company ID / tax identifier (if you enter it), activity profiles.
Content you create: price lists, templates, offers, invoices (if available on your plan), PDFs, notes and client details you enter in order to generate a document.
Purchases: subscription status and entitlements via Google Play Billing (product, term, state). We do not store full payment card or bank account details — payments are processed by Google Play.
Access and limits: trial period, Premium status, Grace Period, offer and PDF export counters where applicable.
Settings: language, currency, theme, PDF language and similar preferences stored in the App.
Technical: basic diagnostics, security, crash information and operational logs needed to run the service.
Client details in offers (name, address, phone, email) are entered by you. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to use them. We store them as part of your content in order to provide the service.

**3. Why we use the data**

We process data in order to:

provide the App — account, saving your work, generating PDFs;
authenticate you (email/password or Google Sign-In) and protect the account;
enable trial, Premium, Grace Period and other paid features;
send service notifications related to your account and offers;
improve reliability and security and prevent abuse;
comply with legal obligations and respond to lawful requests from competent authorities.
We do not sell personal data and do not use it to sell advertising profiles to third parties.

**4. Legal bases (where GDPR applies)**

Where Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) applies, processing is based on one or more of the following:

Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — providing the service you request: account, offers, PDF, subscription;
Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) — security, abuse prevention, stability, administration;
Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) — where required (e.g. accepting terms, certain optional features); you may withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal;
Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) — where the law requires us to retain or disclose data.

**5. Sharing**

We use trusted infrastructure to run the service. The main processors are:

Google Firebase — Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Storage, Cloud Functions (account, content, files, subscription verification);
Google Play Billing — purchases and subscriptions in the Android app;
Google Sign-In — when you choose to sign in with Google.
These providers process data on our instructions and under their own terms and policies. We do not sell personal data.

Content you choose to share (for example a PDF sent to a client) leaves the App under your control.

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect the rights, security and integrity of the service and its users.

**6. International transfers**

Google infrastructure may process data outside the European Economic Area. Where GDPR applies, we rely on appropriate safeguards provided by the vendor (including standard contractual clauses where applicable).

**7. Retention and security**

Data is kept while the account is active and thereafter as needed for security, dispute resolution and legal obligations. After account deletion we delete or anonymise personal data within a reasonable period, except where the law requires longer retention.

We apply technical and organisational measures against unauthorised access (including authentication, database access rules and limited administrative access). No online service can guarantee absolute security.

**8. Your rights and deletion**

Under applicable law (including GDPR where it applies) you may request:

access to your personal data;
rectification of inaccurate data;
erasure (“right to be forgotten”) where the legal grounds are met;
restriction of processing;
portability of data you provided to us, in a structured, commonly used format, where applicable;
objection to processing based on legitimate interests.
To delete your account and associated personal data, use Settings in the App (if available) or contact us via the developer email on the Google Play listing for Master Offer Pro. We will handle the request within a reasonable time.

You have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Bulgaria this is the Commission for Personal Data Protection (CPDP / КЗЛД).

**9. Children**

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us so we can remove it.

**10. Local data on the device**

The App may store settings locally (language, theme, session). This is needed for the service to work. We do not use third-party advertising cookies to sell profiles.

**11. Changes**

We may update this Policy when the service or the law changes. The “Last updated” date will change. For material changes we will aim to notify you in the App. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated Policy to the extent permitted by law.
